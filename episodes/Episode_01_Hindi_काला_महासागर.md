## Animation Notes for Studio

- **Color Palette:** Deep blues, blacks, neon purples for creatures
- **Aren's eyes:** Always show human intelligence, even as dolphin
- **Action sequences:** Fast-paced, dynamic angles, slow-motion on key moments
- **Mana effects:** Glowing particles, energy distortions
- **Sound:** Underwater acoustics, system alerts, emotional piano in dramatic moments
