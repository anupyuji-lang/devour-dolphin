# Devour Dolphin - Story Bible

## Overview
**Title:** Devour Dolphin  
**Genre:** LitRPG, Progression Fantasy, Post-Apocalyptic  
**Tone:** Dark, Action-packed, with moments of hope and humor  
**Target Audience:** 16+  

---

## Core Premise

Aren, an ordinary high school student, dies heroically during humanity's first rescue mission against apocalyptic monsters. Instead of eternal rest, he receives an Infinite Devour System and awakens as a dolphin in a flooded, monster-infested world.

His power: **Absorb any creature's abilities permanently by devouring them.**

His curse: **He must consume to survive and grow, or fade into obscurity.**

His goal: **Become the apex predator of an apocalyptic ocean and protect what remains of humanity.**

---

## World-Building

### The Apocalypse (Day 0)
- **Trigger:** A massive dimensional tear opens in the sky over the Pacific Ocean
- **Cascade:** Seven tears open globally within 24 hours
- **Immediate Effects:**
  - Mana floods Earth's atmosphere for the first time
  - Monsters pour through portals: creatures of nightmare, ancient beasts, dimensional anomalies
  - Oceans rise by 200 feet; coastal cities submerged
  - Electromagnetic systems fail; technology becomes unreliable
  - Governments collapse within weeks
  
### Geography (12 Months Post-Apocalypse)
- **Sunken Cities:** Drowned metropolitan areas become underwater labyrinths
- **The Black Ocean:** Waters turned dark by dimensional contamination; visibility poor, toxicity high
- **Monster Zones:** Specific regions controlled by apex predators
  - Coral Abyss (Poison creatures)
  - Trench of the Leviathan (Ancient zone, forbidden)
  - Storm Front (Where black storms spawn)
- **Human Settlements:** Small island communities, underground bunkers, floating fortresses
- **Safe Zones:** Rare areas where mana concentration is low (shrinking daily)

### Mana System
- **What is Mana?** Fundamental energy that powers magic, mutations, and monster evolution
- **Effect on Humans:** 90% of humans cannot consciously use mana; exposure causes illness or death over time
- **Effect on Animals:** Instant mutation; extreme power gain but loss of intelligence (unless system-guided like Aren)
- **Effect on Monsters:** Natural superiority; they feed on mana to grow stronger
- **Devour System Exception:** Allows selective absorption without uncontrolled mutation

## The Devour System

### Core Mechanics
**Activation:** When Aren consumes (eats, absorbs, or ingests) any living creature with mana, the system activates

**Process:**
1. Creature's body enters Aren's system
2. System scans for extractable traits (abilities, physical enhancements, resistances)
3. Aren receives a choice menu (accept/reject each trait)
4. Selected traits permanently integrate into his being
5. System grades obtained power (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary)

**Limitations:**
- Must consume the *entire* creature (or significant portion) for full absorption
- Can only devour one creature per 2-hour cycle initially
- Traits that conflict cause "Integration Conflicts" (painful, temporary)
- Cannot devour humans (system rejects them with violent backlash)
- Each devour requires 10x normal caloric intake to complete

### Devour Grades
| Grade | Power Level | Evolution Points | Rarity |
|-------|------------|-----------------|--------|
| Common | +1 ability, +5% stat boost | 1 | 60% |
| Uncommon | +2 abilities, +10% stat boost | 3 | 25% |
| Rare | +3 abilities, +20% stat boost, unique skill | 5 | 10% |
| Epic | +4 abilities, +35% stat boost, passive effect | 10 | 4% |
| Legendary | Full power package, new form unlock | 25 | 1% |

### Evolution Stages
1. **Weak Dolphin** (Start) — Basic abilities only
2. **Apex Predator** (10 Rare devours) — Enhanced speed, strength, intelligence
3. **Hybrid Form** (25 Epic devours) — Can transform partially into devoured creatures
4. **Abyss King** (Final, 100+ devours) — Full power realization, continental-scale abilities

### System Corruption (Plot Device)
- The system is intentionally "broken" by an unknown entity before Aren receives it
- It lacks safety limiters and moral constraints
- Side Effect: Aren slowly loses human memories and gains predatory instincts
- Ultimate Question: Will he remain human enough to save humanity?

## Character Profiles

### Aren (Main Character)
**Human Form:** 16-year-old high school student, kind, average athleticism  
**Dolphin Form:** Bottlenose dolphin, 7 feet long, silver-blue coloration

**Personality:**
- Initially: Confused, scared, survivor's guilt
- Mid-story: Confident, cold, predatory
- Late-story: Struggles between humanity and power

**Backstory:**
- Lived ordinary life before apocalypse
- Part of first organized rescue mission (failed)
- Sacrificed himself to save a group of civilians
- Dies heroically; wakes as dolphin

**Arc:**
- Acceptance → Survival → Power → Corruption → Redemption(?)

**Key Relationships:**
- Mira (see below) — His anchor to humanity
- The Leviathan — His destined opponent
- The System — His tool and potential master

---

### Mira (Supporting, Love Interest)
**Age:** 15-16  
**Background:** Survivor of a coastal city, lives in Island Settlement #7

**Personality:**
- Intelligent, observant, compassionate despite trauma
- Skeptical of "monsters" having consciousness
- Changed by recognizing Aren's human eyes in dolphin form

**Story Function:**
- Represents Aren's connection to humanity
- Provides moral compass when he loses it
- Her fate drives climactic decision

**Appearance:** Dark hair, small frame, ocean-green eyes, always carries a journal

---

### The Leviathan (Antagonist)
**Species:** Ancient dimensional creature, continent-sized  
**Form:** Serpentine dragon with multiple heads, bioluminescent markings  

**Nature:**
- Not evil, but *alien* — motivated by dimensional hunger
- Actively consuming Earth's mana to tear the veil further
- Each movement causes earthquakes; its breath creates tsunamis
- Feeds on entire ecosystems

**Significance:**
- Represents the true scale of apocalypse
- No military can defeat it; only Aren's evolved form stands a chance
- Its presence accelerates the world's end

---

### General Thorn (Antagonist)
**Role:** Military commander of human resistance  
**Personality:** Pragmatic, ruthless, sees all monsters as threats  

**Conflict with Aren:**
- Captures/hunts Aren early, believing him dangerous
- Represents humanity's inability to adapt
- Dies defending against Leviathan, acknowledging Aren's necessity

---

### Echo (Supporting Character)
**Species:** Mutated Orca (semi-intelligent)  
**Role:** Aren's first true friend as a dolphin  

**Character:**
- Cannot speak but communicates through clicks and patterns
- Died in Episode 6; motivates Aren's power climb
- Shows dolphins can retain some individuality post-mutation

## Power Progression Arc

### Episode 1-2: Weak Dolphin
- Aren learns basic dolphin abilities
- First hunts: small fish and crabs
- Encounters: Mutated Shark (devours it)
- **Key Power Obtained:** Sharp Teeth, Iron Skin, Water Dash

### Episode 3-4: Apex Predator
- Becomes territorial predator in local waters
- Meets Mira; begins protecting human settlements
- Hunts: Poison Jellyfish, Ghost Rays, Deep-Sea Crabs
- **Key Power Obtained:** Venom Immunity, Echolocation Mastery, Pressure Resistance

### Episode 5-7: Monster Hunter
- Joins human settlements (cautiously accepted)
- Explores sunken cities for powerful monsters
- Hunts: Leviathan Spawn, Ancient Sharks, Dimension Rifters
- **Key Power Obtained:** Telepathy (limited), Energy Absorption, Partial Transformation

### Episode 8-10: Corruption Arc
- Realizes he's becoming less human after 50+ devours
- Loses empathy, gains predatory obsession
- Mira confronts him; he nearly attacks her
- **Key Crisis:** System's broken nature revealed

### Episode 11-12: Abyss King
- Final evolution unlocked
- Becomes god-tier predator
- Challenges Leviathan directly
- **Resolution:** Victory, but at cost of humanity

---

## Thematic Elements

1. **Survival vs. Humanity** — At what point does survival strip away what makes us human?
2. **Power Corruption** — Does great power necessarily corrupt? Or does it reveal truth?
3. **Identity Crisis** — Can consciousness remain intact when biology changes?
4. **Hope in Darkness** — Small moments of connection (Mira, Echo) against apocalyptic scale
5. **Natural Predator** — Accepting nature vs. fighting it; predator vs. person

---

## Tone & Style

- **Action:** High-stakes, visceral, detailed combat
- **Horror:** Atmospheric dread of flooded world, alien creatures
- **Character:** Internal monologues on identity and morality
- **Humor:** Dark, gallows humor from human survivors
- **Hope:** Moments where small acts matter despite apocalypse

---

## Plot Structure

**Act 1:** Awakening (Episodes 1-3)
- Acceptance of dolphin form
- Learning to survive
- First major power gain

**Act 2:** Integration (Episodes 4-7)
- Meeting Mira and humans
- Becoming a protector
- Exploring monster-infested world

**Act 3:** Corruption (Episodes 8-10)
- Increasing power and losing humanity
- Moral crisis and confrontation
- System's true nature revealed

**Act 4:** Apotheosis (Episodes 11-12)
- Final evolution
- Ultimate battle
- Resolution of identity question

---

## Writing Guidelines

- **POV:** Third-person limited (Aren's perspective)
- **Dolphin Perspective:** Use animal instinct but maintain human thought
- **Combat:** Detailed, visceral, show consequences
- **Mana:** Describe as feeling/pressure, not just mechanics
- **Dialogue:** Sparse underwater; use echolocation clicks for flavor
- **Pacing:** Action sequences broken by character introspection
